# Curriculum subject coverage

This audit compares the hand-maintained catalog with the common state curriculum established by Spanish Royal Decrees 157/2022 (Primary) and 217/2022 (ESO). Autonomous communities can add co-official languages, split areas, schedule subjects differently, and extend optional provision, so this is a national baseline rather than a complete regional matrix.

## Primary

The catalog now contains at least one module for every common core area at stage level: Knowledge of the Natural, Social and Cultural Environment; Art Education; Physical Education; Spanish Language and Literature; Foreign Language; and Mathematics. Civic and Ethical Values, required in one third-cycle course, is also represented.

Remaining expansion work is about depth and local provision rather than a wholly absent common area:

- Create balanced coverage for every area in every grade, especially grade 1.
- Add co-official Language and Literature mappings where applicable.
- Add a second foreign language where an education authority or school offers it.
- Treat Religion and its non-confessional alternative according to the applicable school and regional rules.
- Distinguish Music and Plastic/Visual Education when a regional curriculum teaches them separately.

## ESO

Present in the catalog: Mathematics, Spanish Language and Literature, Geography and History, Biology and Geology, and Physics and Chemistry.

Common subjects still without a dedicated ESO module:

- Physical Education.
- Foreign Language.
- Music.
- Plastic, Visual and Audiovisual Education.
- Technology and Digitalisation.
- Civic and Ethical Values.

Fourth-year and minimum optional-offer gaps include Digitalisation; Economy and Entrepreneurship; Latin; Personal and Professional Guidance; Classical Culture; a digital-competence option; and a Second Foreign Language. Exact fourth-year choices and optional subjects must be modelled by autonomous community before claiming complete coverage.

## Product implication

Stage-level presence does not prove curriculum completion. Coverage reporting should track `stage + grade + subject + curriculum strand`, educator-review status, and the autonomous community whose decree was used.
